Terms of Service

Effective date: January 1, 2025

1. Acceptance of Terms

By creating an account with EcoPoints, you confirm that you are a resident of Barangay Dela Paz, Pasig City, and that you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service. If you do not agree, please do not register.

2. Eligibility

The EcoPoints program is open to residents of Barangay Dela Paz, Pasig City only. You must provide accurate personal information during registration, including your real name, valid Gmail address, mobile number, and residential address within the covered streets.

3. Your Account

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials secure. Do not share your password with others. All activity under your account is your responsibility. Report any unauthorized access to the barangay office immediately.

4. EcoPoints Earning

EcoPoints are credited only after a barangay officer verifies your recyclable waste submission. Points are non-transferable, cannot be exchanged for cash, and have no monetary value outside the program. The barangay reserves the right to adjust or revoke points in cases of fraud or misrepresentation.

5. Rewards Redemption

Accumulated EcoPoints may be redeemed for available rewards such as rice, groceries, or school supplies, subject to availability and the barangay's current redemption schedule. Rewards are subject to change without prior notice.

6. Prohibited Conduct

You agree not to: submit false or inflated quantities of recyclables, create multiple accounts, attempt to manipulate the points system, or use the platform for any purpose other than participation in the barangay recycling program.

7. Termination

The barangay reserves the right to suspend or delete any account found to be in violation of these terms, resulting in forfeiture of all accumulated points.

8. Changes to Terms

These terms may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.

9. Contact

For questions, visit the Barangay Dela Paz hall or contact the barangay office directly.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2025

1. Information We Collect

When you register, we collect your first and last name, Gmail address, mobile number, house number, street, and a securely hashed version of your password. We do not store your password in plain text.

2. How We Store Your Data

All registration and session data is stored locally on your device using your browser's localStorage. No data is transmitted to external servers. Your information never leaves your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to identify you within the EcoPoints program, credit points to your account after verified submissions, and allow you to redeem rewards at the barangay level.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties. Your data may only be accessed by authorized barangay officers administering the program.

5. Data Security

Your password is protected using cryptographic hashing with a unique salt. Even if someone accesses your device's storage, your actual password cannot be recovered from the stored data.

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by visiting the barangay office. You also have the right to access and correct any inaccurate information on your profile.

7. Children's Privacy

This program is intended for adults and registered household representatives. If a minor's information is submitted, it must be done by a parent or legal guardian.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any significant changes will be communicated through the barangay's official channels.

9. Contact

For privacy concerns, please visit the Barangay Dela Paz hall or speak with the barangay administrator.
