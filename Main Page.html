Recycle Guide

Know what goes where

A quick look at each material category and what to set aside for collection day.

Paper

Newspapers & Cardboard

Accepted: newspapers, magazines, cardboard boxes, clean paper bags, office paper.

Not accepted: greasy pizza boxes, tissue, wax-coated paper.

Plastic

Bottles & Containers

Accepted: water bottles, shampoo bottles, food containers, rinsed plastic packaging.

Not accepted: plastic wrappers, styrofoam, soiled containers.

Metal

Cans & Scrap Metal

Accepted: aluminum cans, tin cans, metal scraps, bottle caps, clean foil.

Not accepted: aerosol cans, sharp or rusted metal, batteries.

Glass

Bottles & Jars

Accepted: glass bottles, jars, rinsed and with lids removed.

Not accepted: broken glass, mirrors, ceramics, light bulbs.

E-Waste

Gadgets & Cables

Accepted: old phones, chargers, cables, small appliances, batteries (separated).

Not accepted: items with cracked screens leaking fluid, large appliances.

Mixed

Anything You Missed

Accepted: any of the above categories you weren't able to bring earlier in the week.

Please still sort by material before drop-off.
